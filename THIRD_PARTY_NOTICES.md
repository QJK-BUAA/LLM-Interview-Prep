# Third-party learning sources

The extended curriculum is informed by and adapts the organization and topics of
[LLM Post-Training & Agentic RL](https://github.com/XavierZhang2002/agentic-rl-analysis),
by Xavier (zhenliang) and the Agentic RL Analysis Contributors, at commit
`66ae4423b36270ef50a288fb1bb2e1b31c46c329`. The lessons are rewritten for beginners
and checked against the primary sources listed in each chapter. Source images
are not redistributed.
